Hotels

Suggestions for places to stay while you are here – a curated collection of characterful hotels, boutique retreats and luxury resorts across India.

South India comprises of the states of Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Goa and the islands of Lakshadweep and Andaman & Nicobar. Each state has its own language and cuisine, and the people have distinct customs that vary even within each state.

The region is naturally endowed with fertile lowlands, long coastal stretches, dense forests, high mountains and vast plateaus. Its architectural grandeur is reflected in magnificent domes and minarets, sprawling temple complexes with beautiful carvings and giant statues, and charming colonial‑era quarters.

Below is a selection of hotels and retreats that we regularly work with – from heritage mansions and boutique bungalows to contemporary luxury resorts – chosen for their character, comfort and location.
