Why Travel With Us

A Captivating India holiday helps you experience the real India without worrying about organisational detail. Your journey is infused with the true flavour of India through its great diversity and closer contact with local people.

Our tours typically include the major sites and monuments, countryside and villages, perhaps a wildlife reserve and time to relax at a beach or mountain resort. You can tailor this balance to suit your needs. The experience is enhanced by a variety of accommodation, modes of transport and, where possible, local cultural events.

Commitment to high standards and specialist knowledge, coupled with our passion for India, ensures quality holidays of distinction. Many of our guests consider their time with us one of the best holidays they have ever taken.

We take you to busy bazaars and colourful markets, to villages where craftspeople practise ancient skills, into schools with wide‑eyed children and into local family homes where you may experience their lifestyle and even join them for a traditional meal.

You stay in a carefully chosen mix of luxury hotels, boutique properties, heritage homes and characterful retreats. Our selection is based on comfort, quality of service and the personality of each property, with the benefit of detailed research and guest feedback.

Why travel with Captivating India:

  • Enjoy high quality of service at competitive prices compared with similar holidays elsewhere.
  • Work with a team that understands both Western expectations and Indian hospitality, able to design the perfect holiday around your requirements.
  • Have any problems dealt with swiftly and efficiently, with 24×7 on‑call support.
  • Benefit from access to lesser‑known sights, restaurants and events through our ongoing on‑the‑ground research.
  • Have a dedicated member of our team assigned to your holiday to ensure every detail is understood and delivered.
  • Travel with well‑trained drivers and well‑maintained vehicles for a safe, memorable journey.

Backed by first‑hand local knowledge, attention to detail and flawless arrangements, a Captivating India holiday is designed to be the holiday of a lifetime.
