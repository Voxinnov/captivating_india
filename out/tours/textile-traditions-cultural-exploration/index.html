Tour

Textile traditions & a cultural exploration

Tamil Nadu, Karnataka & Kerala

Overview

South India, with its rich history, diverse traditions and vibrant artisan communities, offers a unique and immersive journey for travellers interested in textiles and cultural heritage. This tour is not just a showcase of exquisite fabrics and intricate weaving techniques, but also a deep dive into living traditions, temple architecture, cuisine and the daily life of communities in Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and Kerala.

It is a popular journey for guests who would like a complete experience across three states. You not only see the textile methods practised in each region but also experience how diverse they are in their way of life, culture and cuisine. Do get in touch for more details or to have a customised trip arranged to suit your specific interests.
